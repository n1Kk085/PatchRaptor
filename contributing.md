# Contributing Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in **PatchRaptor**!

While this project is **source-available**, we highly value community feedback and suggestions.

## How to Contribute
We prefer to manage all bug reports, feature requests, and support discussions through our Discord community to keep everything in one place.

-   **Bug Reports**: Found a bug? Let us know in the `#support` channel on Discord!
-   **Suggestions**: Have an idea? Post it in `#feedback`.
-   **Support**: Need help setting up? Our community is ready to assist.

**[Join the Discord Server](https://discord.gg/NmHbruVs56)**

> **Note**: We do not accept Pull Requests or track Issues on GitHub at this time.

## "Source-Available" Policy
-   **Commercial Use**: You are free to use PatchRaptor on your servers, including those that generate revenue.
-   **No Redistribution**: You may **NOT** repackage, redistribute, or sell this software (source or binary). Official releases are provided only by the author.
